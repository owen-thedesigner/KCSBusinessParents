Membership Requirements

Membership Requirements

  1. KCS Parent Members must be a parent of a currently enrolled KCS student.
  2. Regular Attendance Members must attend at least one meeting per month. Meetings are held twice monthly on Wednesdays at 8:30 a.m.
  3. Annual Dues Annual membership dues are $100. These funds are used primarily to partner with KCS through sponsorships and support of school programs, including KCS Athletics and KCS Fine Arts. A small portion supports the group's website, which maintains business listings for all active members.

Membership Status

Members who do not meet these requirements will have their membership temporarily suspended until the outstanding requirements are fulfilled.
